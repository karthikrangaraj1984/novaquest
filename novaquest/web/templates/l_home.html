What do these numbers mean?
  • Active this week: students who opened a lesson, practised, took a test or played an escape room in the last 7 days.
  • Understanding: the average score on practice, understanding checks, lesson quizzes and tests in the last 4 weeks. It is a learning indicator, not an exam mark.
  • Handed in on time: of every student an assignment was set for, the share who handed it in by its deadline, over assignments that closed in the last 8 weeks.
  • AI skills: how well students open escape-room locks for each AI skill (first try without a hint scores highest).
  • A figure needs at least {{ data.min_students }} students behind it; below that it says "Not enough data".
